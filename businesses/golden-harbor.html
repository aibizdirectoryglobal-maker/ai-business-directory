Overview

About the business

Golden Harbor Consultancy is a Malaysia-based independent loan consultancy supporting personal and SME/business loan applicants. Its official website describes eligibility assessment, document preparation, application support, and CCRIS and CTOS report guidance. Mortgage Refinancing Advisory, Debt Consolidation Advisory and the Klang Valley service area are also business-advertised on Golden Harbor's official website. This information is publicly documented but not independently verified.

Services and advisory scope

  • Application review

    Review of income, credit history, and existing documents before submission.

  • Document preparation

    Help organising documents against a chosen bank’s requirements.

  • Application guidance

    Comparison of published eligibility requirements and support preparing follow-up enquiries.

  • Credit report guidance

    Help understanding CCRIS and CTOS reports and identifying possible errors to query.

  • Mortgage Refinancing Advisory

    Guidance on refinancing existing housing loans, subject to lender eligibility and approval.

    Business-advertised on Golden Harbor's official website. Publicly documented; not independently verified.

  • Debt Consolidation Advisory

    Guidance on exploring financing arrangements to consolidate existing debts. Debt consolidation is a financing objective, not necessarily a standalone loan product. Savings and approval are not guaranteed. This advisory service does not offer removal of accurate credit records.

    Business-advertised on Golden Harbor's official website. Publicly documented advisory scope; not independently verified.

Understanding its role

Golden Harbor states that it is not a bank or lender. Loan approval remains with each bank and is not guaranteed.
