Overview

About the business

ASM describes itself as a financial and bank-loan advisory agency. Its published scope includes application-document support, financial consultancy, debt-repayment planning and client profiling. These are the business’s own descriptions, not independently verified capabilities.

Business-advertised scope: ASM’s official business and advisory description.

Services and advertised scope

The following descriptions summarise ASM’s published claims. Delivery, eligibility and outcomes have not been independently verified.

  • Personal financing

    ASM advertises personal-financing support. Its personal-loan page discusses credit-profile assessment and borrowing purposes that include existing debts. Eligibility and financing terms require confirmation.

    ASM’s personal-financing descriptions

  • SME/business financing

    ASM advertises working-capital facilities and commercial-property financing or refinancing. Published product descriptions should not be treated as confirmed offers available to every applicant.

    ASM’s SME financing descriptions

  • Mortgage refinancing

    ASM describes help reviewing refinancing, renewal and mortgage-switching options. Any applicable offer and terms require direct confirmation.

    ASM’s mortgage refinancing descriptions

  • Debt consolidation

    ASM discusses consolidation through mortgage/home-equity arrangements and personal borrowing. Its contact form also accepts consolidation enquiries. The financing arrangement and programme terms are unconfirmed here. No savings or approval guarantee is made by this directory.

    Mortgage and home-equity descriptions, personal borrowing descriptions and consolidation enquiry categories.

  • Debt-management support

    Repayment planning is advertised in ASM’s business description; debt-management enquiries appear on its contact form. Detailed processes and eligibility are unconfirmed.

    ASM’s repayment-planning description and debt-management enquiry categories.

    The contact form accepts credit-repair enquiries involving AKPK, low CTOS and SAA. This does not establish affiliation, a particular outcome or the ability to remove accurate records.

    ASM’s published credit-profile enquiry categories.
