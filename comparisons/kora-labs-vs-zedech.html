Questions to confirm before engaging either provider

These questions arise from gaps, qualifications and project-specific conditions in the reviewed public information.

  • Project scope and deliverables. Which workflows, integrations, deliverables and exclusions will the written scope cover, and how are changes priced?
  • Acceptance testing. Which test cases and pass criteria apply, who signs off, and what defect-fixing period is included?
  • Total implementation and recurring costs. What are the implementation, hosting, model/API, licensing, maintenance and change-request charges, payment triggers and cancellation terms?
  • Ownership and data export. Who owns custom code and reusable components, who controls accounts and repositories, and how can data and documentation be exported on exit?
  • Support commitments. What support hours, response targets, monitoring, backups and recovery responsibilities are included in writing?
  • Security and third-party integrations. What customer data reaches each platform or AI provider, what permissions and retention apply, and which actions require human approval?
